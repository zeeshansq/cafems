How Misc Charges Apply to Monthly Bills

Monthly Misc Charges are automatically included in an employee's invoice ONLY IF the employee has issued at least 1 Lunch Token during that month. If an employee was absent or had 0 lunch tokens (e.g. only roti or sweet without a lunch token), the Misc Charge is automatically waived / excluded from their bill.
